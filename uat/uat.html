Saved

AssetCore is an asset-management and maintenance system for Nigeria Gas Marketing Limited (NGML). This round walks the full spine — inspection to finding to defect to work order — then the newer modules that shipped in Phases 1-4 and have had the least real use: approvals, escalations, compliance audits, the risk register, depreciation and currency. Every test names a real screen, a real account and a real button. Where a test expects a refusal, the refusal is the pass.
